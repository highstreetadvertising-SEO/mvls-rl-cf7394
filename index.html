Frequently Asked Questions About Utility Locating in 2026

1. What is utility locating and why is it important?

Utility locating is the process of identifying and marking the positions of underground utilities — including gas lines, water pipes, electrical conduits, sewer lines, and telecommunications cables — before excavation begins. It is critically important in 2026 because striking an unmarked underground line can cause service disruptions, property damage, serious injuries, and significant legal liability. Professional utility locating protects workers, property, and the surrounding community.

2. Is calling 811 enough before I dig in Buckley, WA?

No. While calling 811 is legally required and a mandatory first step, it only covers publicly owned utility lines. Private utilities, service laterals, irrigation systems, and other privately owned underground infrastructure are not marked through the 811 system. For comprehensive protection, you should always hire a professional utility locating service like Mt. View Locating Services in addition to completing your 811 notification.

3. What technology is used in professional utility locating?

Professional utility locating companies in 2026 use two primary technologies: electromagnetic (EM) locating, which detects conductive utilities like metal pipes and electrical lines, and ground-penetrating radar (GPR), which uses radio waves to detect both conductive and non-conductive utilities including PVC pipes and other plastic materials. The most accurate and comprehensive providers, like Mt. View Locating Services, use both technologies in combination.

4. How long does a utility locate take?

The duration of a utility locate depends on the size and complexity of the site. A standard residential utility locate can typically be completed in one to two hours. Larger commercial or industrial sites with complex utility networks may require a full day or multiple visits. Your locating provider should give you a time estimate during the scheduling process.

5. How much does utility locating cost in Buckley, WA?

Utility locating costs in Buckley, WA in 2026 vary based on the size of the project, the number of utilities to be located, the technology required, and the specific service provider. Residential locates typically start in the range of a few hundred dollars, while larger commercial projects can range higher. The cost of professional locating is always minimal compared to the expense of repairing a damaged utility line or managing injury-related liability.

6. Can utility locating find all types of underground lines?

Professional utility locating using both GPR and EM technology can detect the vast majority of underground utilities. However, no locating method is 100% guaranteed to find every line in every condition — particularly very old, abandoned, or poorly documented infrastructure. This is why comprehensive documentation from your utility provider and a detailed site consultation are essential components of a thorough utility locating service.

7. What certifications should I look for in a utility locating company?

In 2026, look for companies whose technicians hold certifications from the American Public Works Association (APWA) through their Utility and Damage Prevention program. Additional relevant credentials include NASSCO certifications, state-specific licensing, and membership in professional organizations like the Common Ground Alliance (CGA). These credentials indicate that technicians have received formal training and adhere to industry best practices.

8. What's the difference between private utility locating and public utility locating?

Public utility locating refers to the marking of publicly owned infrastructure — typically handled through the 811 system where individual utility companies send their own locators. Private utility locating covers infrastructure owned by private parties, including service laterals, private water lines, irrigation systems, and interior building utilities. Professional services like Mt. View Locating Services specialize in private utility locating to fill the critical gaps left by the public 811 system.

9. How far in advance should I schedule a utility locate?

In 2026, with construction activity at a high level throughout Pierce County and the greater Puget Sound region, it is advisable to schedule your utility locate as early as possible — ideally one to two weeks before your planned excavation date. Many providers can accommodate faster scheduling for urgent situations, including same-day or next-day service, but availability during peak construction season can be limited.

10. Can utility locating help with landscaping and fence installation projects?

Absolutely. Utility locating is just as important for smaller residential projects like landscaping, fence installation, deck construction, and tree removal as it is for large commercial excavations. Underground utilities can be shallower than expected, and even routine digging can strike a gas or electrical line with serious consequences. Always verify what's underground before any digging, regardless of project scale.

11. What happens if a utility line is accidentally struck during excavation?

If a utility line is struck during excavation, the consequences can include service outages for entire neighborhoods, fire or explosion risk from gas line strikes, electrical shock hazards, flooding from broken water mains, and substantial repair costs. The excavating party may also face legal liability and regulatory fines. Professional utility locating significantly reduces this risk, making it one of the most important investments you can make before starting any underground work.
